Privacy Policy

Video Autopost is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos.

What data is used: the access tokens issued by TikTok and Instagram after the owner signs in, the basic profile info (display name), and the statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares).

How it is used: only to upload videos the owner sends, and to build a private statistics report for the owner.

Storage: data is stored on the owner's private server and is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok or Instagram settings; all stored data is deleted together with the server database.
